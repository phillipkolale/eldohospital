About Eldo Hospital

Facilities, staff & doctors

Care built on integrated clinical practice, education and research.

Our facilities are built to give patients a calm, modern environment to recover in — clean, well-equipped, and staffed by people who take the time to explain what's happening and why.

Diagnostic and treatment equipment is kept current, so testing stays accurate and diagnosis stays fast.

Staff work in two shifts to keep every department covered around the clock, drawn from cardiology, neurology, dentistry and beyond — selected from strong training backgrounds and screened for how they actually treat patients, not just their qualifications on paper.
